# 🌐 NetLab UZ

### Tarmoq texnologiyalari va kiberxavfsizlikni amaliy o‘rganish platformasi

## 🇺🇿 Loyiha haqida

**NetLab UZ** — tarmoq texnologiyalari, kiberxavfsizlik va axborot texnologiyalarini o‘rganishga mo‘ljallangan ta’lim platformasi.

Loyihaning asosiy yo‘nalishi — nazariy bilimlarni amaliy mashg‘ulotlar bilan birlashtirish. Platforma foydalanuvchilarga kompyuter tarmoqlari, operatsion tizimlar, axborot xavfsizligi va zamonaviy IT texnologiyalarini bosqichma-bosqich o‘rganish imkonini beradi.

NetLab UZ oddiy o‘quv materiallari to‘plami emas, balki bilim olish, tajriba o‘tkazish va amaliy ko‘nikmalarni rivojlantirish uchun yaratilayotgan interaktiv muhitdir.

## 🎯 Loyihaning maqsadi

NetLab UZ loyihasining asosiy maqsadi — O‘zbekistonda IT, tarmoq texnologiyalari va kiberxavfsizlik sohalariga qiziquvchi yoshlar uchun qulay va amaliy ta’lim muhitini yaratish.

Loyiha quyidagi vazifalarni amalga oshirishga qaratilgan:

- IT bilimlarini tizimli va bosqichma-bosqich o‘rgatish.
- Tarmoq texnologiyalarini amaliy misollar orqali tushuntirish.
- Kiberxavfsizlik bo‘yicha boshlang‘ich bilimlarni rivojlantirish.
- Virtual laboratoriyalar va amaliy topshiriqlar yaratish.
- Mustaqil o‘rganish va tajriba qilish imkoniyatini kengaytirish.
- O‘zbek tilidagi sifatli IT ta’lim resurslarini rivojlantirish.

## 🧪 Asosiy yo‘nalishlar

- **Learn** — nazariy bilimlar va o‘quv materiallari.
- **Lab** — amaliy mashg‘ulotlar va virtual laboratoriyalar.
- **Operation** — amaliy vaziyatlar va bilimlarni sinash.

## 💡 Loyiha g‘oyasi

Bizning fikrimizcha, IT sohasini o‘rganish faqat nazariy ma’lumotlarni o‘qish bilan cheklanmasligi kerak.

Haqiqiy bilim tajriba, izlanish va amaliyot orqali shakllanadi.

**NetLab UZ — o‘rganishdan amaliyotga, amaliyotdan tajribaga.**

## 🇬🇧 About the Project

**NetLab UZ** is an educational platform designed for learning networking, cybersecurity, and information technology.

The project combines theoretical knowledge with practical learning experiences, helping users understand computer networks, operating systems, information security, and modern IT technologies.

NetLab UZ aims to provide an interactive environment where students can learn, experiment, and develop practical technical skills.

## 🎯 Project Goals

The main goal of NetLab UZ is to create an accessible and practical learning environment for young people interested in IT, networking, and cybersecurity in Uzbekistan.

Key objectives:

- Provide structured IT learning resources.
- Teach networking through practical examples.
- Introduce cybersecurity fundamentals.
- Develop virtual labs and practical exercises.
- Encourage independent learning and experimentation.
- Support Uzbek-language IT education.

## 🧪 Main Sections

- **Learn** — educational materials and theory.
- **Lab** — practical exercises and virtual laboratories.
- **Operation** — practical scenarios and knowledge assessment.

## 💡 Our Vision

We believe that learning IT should go beyond reading theory.

Real knowledge comes from practice, experimentation, and continuous learning.

**NetLab UZ — From Learning to Practice, From Practice to Experience.**
